<p>There will be an invited talk</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Title: <strong>Uncertainty quantification for kinetic equations</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Speaker: <strong>Shi Jin</strong> (University of Wisconsin-Madison, USA and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China)</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Time: <strong>2016 July 25th 14:00 - 15:00</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Place: <strong>129-406</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Abstract:</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Kinetic equations have uncertain inputs, such as the scattering kernels, initial or boundary data.<br />
In this talk we will study the generalized polynomial chaos (gPC) approach to such kinetic equations with multiple time or space scales, </span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">and show that they can be made asymptotic-preserving, in the sense that the gPC scheme preserves various asymptotic limits in the discrete space.<br />
This allows the implemention of the gPC methods for these problems without numerically resolving (by space, time, and gPC modes) the small scales. We also give a fast gPC algorithm for the Boltzmann equation with uncertainties in its collision kernel, initial or boundary data.</span></p>
