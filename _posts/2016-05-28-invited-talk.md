<p>There will be an invited talk.<br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt"><br />
Title: Refined error estimates and decay properties for a damped semilinear wave equation<br />
Speaker: <em>Debora Amadori</em> (University of L’Aquila)<br />
Time: 30th May (Monday) 15:00 - 16:00<br />
Place: 129 - 406<br />
Abstract: <a href="{{ '/assets/uploads/invited/abstract_2016-05_SNU.pdf' | relative_url }}">[Click here]</a></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 9pt">This talk will concern the approximation of a semilinear wave equation with space-dependent damping in one space dimension.<br />
After rewriting the equation as a first order system, we present an approach for proving rigorous L^1 error estimates<br />
for certain classes of approximations. The main relevant features are that these approximations preserve stationary solutions<br />
and that the L^1 difference with exact solutions is bounded uniformly in time, therefore leading to accurate estimates for large times.<br />
Moreover, a decay property of the total variation of the solution is shown in the region where the damping is supported.<br />
This will be related to a well-known property of decay of energy for the damped wave equation.</span></p>
