<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Lecture Topics:<br />
<strong>Prof. Ruggeri:</strong><br />
<strong>Lecture 1,2:</strong> Balance Laws, Hyperbolic Systems, Symmetric Systems. Main field and theorem of symmetrization. Examples of Euler fluids, non-linear elasticity, MHD, Born-Infeld nonlinear electrodynamics, relativistic fluids.<br />
<strong>Lecture 3,4:</strong> Compatibility between Galilean invariance and Entropy Principle. Qualitative analysis, K -condition and global existence of smooth solutions. Principal subsystems and nesting theories with examples. The mixture of gas and connection with Cucker-Smale models.<br />
<strong>Lecture 5,6:</strong> Shock wave and growth of entropy across the shock, shock structure and sub-shocks formation. Problematic of Rational Extended Thermodynamics in mono and polyatomic gas.<br />
