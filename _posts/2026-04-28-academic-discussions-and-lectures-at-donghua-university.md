<p>From April 24 to April 25, Prof. Ha and Chaejoo Lee visited Donghua University in Shanghai. During their visit, they discussed ongoing research projects and possible collaborations.</p>
<p>As part of the visit, Prof. Ha shared recent progress in inertial spin models, and Chaejoo Lee presented a half-hour talk on their recent research.</p>
