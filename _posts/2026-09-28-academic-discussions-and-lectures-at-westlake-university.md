<p>From September 17 to September 24, Prof. Ha, Prof. Zeng, and Chaejoo Lee visited Westlake University in Hangzhou. During the visit, they discussed ongoing research projects and participated in the Mathematical Forum on Control and Learning, where they presented their recent research results.

They also met Prof. Doheon Kim, a former HYKE member, and caught up with each other while exchanging their current research interests.
