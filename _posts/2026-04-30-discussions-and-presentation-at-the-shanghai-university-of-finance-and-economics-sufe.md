<p>From April 25 to April 30, Prof. Ha and Chaejoo Lee visited the Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE) in Shanghai. During their visit, they held discussions on ongoing research projects.</p>
<p>As part of the visit, Prof. Ha delivered an overview lecture on collective dynamics, and Chaejoo Lee presented a short talk on his research topic.</p>
