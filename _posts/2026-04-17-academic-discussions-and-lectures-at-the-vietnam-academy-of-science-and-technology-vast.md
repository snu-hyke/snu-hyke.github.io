<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>From April 12 to April 17, Prof. Ha, Jaemoon Lee, and Chaejoo Lee visited the Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST) in Hanoi. During their visit, they held discussions on ongoing research projects and explored ways to promote academic exchange between the mathematical communities of Korea and Vietnam.</p>
<p>As part of the visit, Prof. Ha delivered an overview lecture on collective dynamics, and Jaemoon Lee and Chaejoo Lee each presented a half-hour talk on their recent research.</p>
