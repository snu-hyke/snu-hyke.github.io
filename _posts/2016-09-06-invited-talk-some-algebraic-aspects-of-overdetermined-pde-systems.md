<p>There will be an invited talk</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Title: <strong>Some algebraic aspects of overdetermined PDE systems</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Speaker: <strong>Prof. Chong Kyu Han (한종규)</strong> </span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Time: <strong>2016 September 30th, October 7th and November 4th 10:00am~11:30am</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Place: <strong>27-116</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Abstract:</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> I will survey the subject starting with examples and the notion of involutivity.<br />
Then we review related topics in generalized Frobenius theorems and the Cartan-Kaehler theory,<br />
Finally, we discuss open problems in the following areas:<br />
1) Conservation laws for boundaries and for submanifolds of higher codimensions,<br />
2) Existence of complex varieties (with singularities) in a real hypersurface of a complex space<br />
3) Applications to control theory.</span></p>
