<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>From May 14 to May 19, Prof. Ha, Dr. Wang and Dr. Zeng visited Harbin Institute of Technology and Harbin Engineering University. During the visit, they discussed ongoing research projects and potential collaborations for collective dynamics.</p>
<p>As a part of visit, Prof. Ha presented an overview lecture on flocking and synchronization, and Dr. Wang and Dr. Zeng gave a half-hour talk on their research.<br />
