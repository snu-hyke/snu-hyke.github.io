<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>From March 22 to March 29, Prof. Ha, Dr. Wang, and Jaemoon Lee visited the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center to continue their collaboration on collective dynamics. In particular, Prof. Ha gave two distinguished lectures (C.C. Lin Distinguished Lecture hosted by the Department of Mathematics, Tsinghua University) on March 25th and March 27th. Moreover, Dr. Wang and Jaemoon Lee also gave one-hour invited lectures at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center.</p>
