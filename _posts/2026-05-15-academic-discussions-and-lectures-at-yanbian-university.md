<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>From May 10 to May 14, Prof. Ha, Myeonghyeon Kim and Chaejoo Lee visited Yanbian University. During the visit, they discussed ongoing research projects and potential collaborations.</p>
<p>As a part of visit, Prof. Ha presented an overview lecture on collective dynamics, and Myeonghyeon Kim and Chaejoo Lee gave a half-hour talk on their recent research.</p>
