<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Title: <strong>On the emergent behaviors of stochastic flocking models.</strong></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Abstract: <strong>We study stochastic alignment of the Justh-Krishnaprasad(J-K) model under the additive and multiplicative noises. Justh and Krishnaprasad proposed a planar Newtonian particle model for an ensemble of flocking agents moving with a unit speed, where the dynamics of the heading angles (of velocities) is governed by a generalized Kuramoto-type model with a communication weight. In this paper, we are interested in the random perturbation of the communication weight, which is represented by the additive and multiplicative noises.</strong></span></p>
