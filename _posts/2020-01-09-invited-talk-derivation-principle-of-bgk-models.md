<p>There will be an invited talk.</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Title: <strong>Derivation principle of BGK models</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Speaker: <strong>Prof. Stephane Brull</strong> (Univ. of Bordeaux)</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Time: <strong>2020 January 14th, 10:30am~11:20pm</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Place: <strong>27-116</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Abstract:</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> In this talk we will present a derivation principle of BGK<br />
models using the resolution of an entropy minimization problem.</p>
<p>The construction is based as on the introduction of relaxation<br />
coefficients and a principle of entropy minimization under<br />
constraints for moments. These free parameters are next ajusted to<br />
transport coefficients when performing a Chapman-Engskog expansion<br />
ip to Navier-Stokes. Firstly, the methodology will be explained and<br />
illustrated for a monoatomic and polyatomic single gas.<br />
Next the case of gas mixtures is considered. In this part, after<br />
clarifying the Chapman-Engskog, a BGK model is derived. This BGK<br />
model is proved to satisfy Fick and Newton laws. In a last part, we<br />
will explain how to extend our model to reacting gas mixtures.</p>
