<p>There will be an invited talk.</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Title: <strong>Green's functions and Well-posedness of Compressible Navier-Stokes equation</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Speaker: <strong>Prof. Shih-Hsien Yu</strong> (National University of Singapore)</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Time: <strong>2018 November 13th, 4:00pm~5:00pm</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Place: <strong>27-220</strong></span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> Abstract:</span><br />
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"> A class of decomposition of Green's functions for the compressilbe Navier-Stokes linearized around a constant state is introduced. The singular structures of the Green's functions are developed as  essential devices to use the nonlinearity directly to covert the 2nd order quasi-linear PDE into a system of zero-th order integral equation with regular integral kernels. The system of integrable equations allows a wider class of functions such as BV solutions. We have shown global existence and well-posedness of the compressible Navier-Stokes equation for isentropic gas with the gas constant γ∈(0,e) in the Lagrangian coordinate for the class of the BV functions and point wise L∞ around a constant state; and the<br />
underline pointwise structure of the solutions is constructed. It is also shown that for the class of BV solution the solution is at most piecewise C2-solution even though the initial data is piecewise C^infty.</span></p>
