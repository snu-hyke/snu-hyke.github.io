<p>From October 9 to October 17, Prof. Ha, Jaemoon Lee, Chaejoo Lee, Eunjun Lee, and Jaehun Jung visited the Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology (VAST) in Hanoi. During their visit, they held discussions on ongoing research projects and explored ways to promote academic exchange between the mathematical communities of Korea and Vietnam.</p>

<p>As part of the visit, Prof. Ha delivered three overview lecture on collective dynamics, and other students each presented an hour talk on their recent research.</p>
